# Store Opening Procedure

Two associates minimum, always. The opening manager arrives 45 minutes before opening; the second associate arrives 30 minutes before.

## Sequence

1. **Exterior check before entering.** Walk the front. Look for forced entry, broken glass, or anything out of place. If something is wrong, do not enter. Call the regional manager and, if warranted, local police from outside the building.
2. **Disarm and log in.** Disarm the alarm with your own code, never a shared one. A shared code makes the access log useless.
3. **Walk the floor with the second associate.** Confirm the building is empty before either person is out of sight of the other.
4. **Count the drawer.** Both associates present, per the cash handling procedure. Float is $300.
5. **Systems check.** Registers online, network up, price changes applied overnight. Report a failed price sync to Retail Systems before opening, not after the first customer complaint.
6. **Safety walk.** Entrance clear, mats down, spills addressed, fitting rooms empty, emergency exits unobstructed.
7. **Merchandising pass.** Front tables faced, sale signage matching current pricing, expired promotional signage removed.

## Weather

Snow and ice clearance at the entrance is the opening manager's responsibility before opening, not after. Document the clearance in the store log with the time. A slip at an entrance with no clearance record is indefensible.

## Opening late

If the store cannot open on time, notify the regional manager and update the store listing. Customers waiting at a locked door with no explanation is the worst outcome.
