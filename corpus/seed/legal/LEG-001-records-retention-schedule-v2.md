# Records Retention Schedule

This schedule is authoritative for how long Kestrel Ridge keeps records. Where a departmental standard states a different period, this schedule governs.

| Record type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Order and sales records | 7 years |
| Tax filings and supporting records | 7 years |
| Employee personnel file | 7 years after separation |
| Workplace investigation records | 7 years |
| Payroll records | 4 years |
| Benefits enrollment records | 6 years |
| Vendor contracts | 7 years after expiration |
| Board minutes | Permanent |
| Customer account (closed) | 3 years after closure |
| Web analytics events | 25 months |
| Application logs | 90 days |
| Access and audit logs | **7 years** |
| Loyalty transaction history | 5 years |

Access and audit log retention increased from 2 years to 7 years in this version, following the compliance program review. Systems still enforcing the 2 year period are out of compliance and must be corrected by June 30, 2026.

## Destruction

Records at end of retention are destroyed on the monthly destruction run. Destruction is suspended for anything covered by a legal hold, and a hold overrides every retention period in this table.

## Where records live

The schedule applies to the record, not the system. A retained record stored in three systems must be destroyed in all three; the owning department is responsible for knowing where its records live.

## Questions

Route retention questions to Legal before deleting anything. Deleting early is the error that cannot be undone.
