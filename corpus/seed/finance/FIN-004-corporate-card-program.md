# Corporate Card Program

Corporate cards are issued to employees who travel more than twice a year or hold purchasing responsibility. Request one through Ledgerline with director approval.

## Limits

| Card tier | Monthly limit | Single transaction limit |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | $5,000 | $2,000 |
| Travel | $15,000 | $7,500 |
| Purchasing | $50,000 | $25,000 |

Limit increases are temporary by default and expire after 60 days unless renewed.

## Reconciliation

Transactions must be reconciled in Ledgerline within 30 days of the statement date. Cards with transactions unreconciled past 60 days are suspended automatically, and reinstatement requires director approval.

## Personal use

The card is for business expenses only. Incidental personal charges must be repaid within 30 days through the repayment link in Ledgerline. Repeated personal use is a code of conduct matter.

## Lost or compromised cards

Report immediately to the issuer at the number on the card, then notify Accounts Payable. Fraudulent charges reported within 60 days carry no liability for the employee.

## Departure

Cards are cancelled through the offboarding workflow. Outstanding reconciliations are completed by the departing employee's manager.
