# Purchase Approval Authority

Approval thresholds apply to the annual value of a purchase.

| Annual value | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $1,000 | Manager |
| $1,001 to $10,000 | Director |
| $10,001 to $50,000 | Vice President |
| Above $50,000 | Chief Financial Officer |

## Software and vendors

Software purchases require a security review only when the vendor stores customer data.

Purchases above $50,000 require two written quotes.

## Purchase orders

Commitments above $25,000 require a purchase order. Renewals do not require re-approval if the value has not increased by more than 10 percent.
