# Vendor Payment Terms

Standard terms for Kestrel Ridge are net 45 from invoice receipt. Vendors requesting shorter terms require controller approval.

| Vendor category | Standard terms | Early payment discount |
|---|---|---|
| Merchandise suppliers | Net 60 | 2% if paid in 15 days |
| Logistics and freight | Net 30 | none |
| Professional services | Net 45 | none |
| Software and subscriptions | Net 45 | 1% annual prepay |
| Facilities and utilities | Due on receipt | none |

Accounts Payable takes the early payment discount automatically when working capital allows; the decision is made in the weekly disbursement review, not by the requesting department.

## Disbursement runs

Payments are released every Friday. A run cutoff of Wednesday 2:00 PM applies; invoices approved after cutoff move to the following week.

Manual or off-cycle payments require controller approval and are reserved for cases where a delay would stop operations.

## New vendors

A vendor must be registered in the vendor register with a completed W-9 and verified banking details before the first payment. Banking detail changes require verbal confirmation with a known contact at a previously recorded phone number, never a number supplied in the change request itself.

## Disputes

Disputed invoices are held in full, not partially paid, and the vendor is notified within 5 business days.
