# Contract Signature Authority

Only the people listed here may bind Kestrel Ridge. Signing without authority does not automatically void the contract, which is exactly why the limits matter.

| Contract type | Signer |
|---|---|
| Purchase of goods or services under $100,000 | Vice President of the requesting function |
| Purchase of goods or services $100,000 and above | Chief Financial Officer |
| Store leases and real estate | Chief Executive Officer |
| Employment offers | Chief People Officer or delegate |
| Merchandise supply agreements | Chief Merchandising Officer |
| Anything with a non-standard indemnity, liability cap, or governing law | General Counsel, in addition to the signer above |

## Before signing

Every contract goes through Legal review before signature. Standard-form agreements on Kestrel Ridge paper with no redlines are reviewed within 3 business days; anything on vendor paper takes 10.

A purchase order is not a contract. A signed vendor quote often is. Read what you are signing.

## Terms Legal will not accept without escalation

Unlimited liability. Automatic renewal longer than 12 months without a notice window. Governing law outside the United States. Any term granting a vendor rights to customer data beyond providing the service.

## Electronic signature

Executed through the contract system, which preserves the audit trail. A contract signed outside that system must be uploaded within 5 business days or it will not be in the contract register, and a contract nobody can find is a contract nobody can enforce.
