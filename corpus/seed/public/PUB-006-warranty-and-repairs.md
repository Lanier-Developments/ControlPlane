# Product Warranty and Repairs

A warranty claim and a return are different things. Returns are about changing your mind within 90 days. Warranties are about something failing when it shouldn't have.

## Kestrel Ridge branded gear

Our own gear carries a limited lifetime warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, for the original owner, with proof of purchase.

Covered: seams that fail, zippers that separate, delamination, hardware that breaks under normal use.

Not covered: wear from ordinary use, damage from accidents or improper care, alterations, and abrasion. A jacket worn for six seasons that finally wears through is not a defect.

## Other brands

Each manufacturer sets its own terms. We'll help you start a claim and tell you what to expect, but the manufacturer makes the decision. Bring the item and your proof of purchase to any store.

## Repairs

We repair what we can rather than replacing it. Minor repairs — zipper sliders, small tears, snap replacement — are done in store, usually within a week.

Larger repairs go to our repair center. Expect 3 to 4 weeks, longer between October and December.

Repairs on covered warranty claims are free. Out-of-warranty repairs are quoted before any work starts, and we'll tell you honestly when a repair costs more than the item is worth.

## Safety gear

Climbing hardware, harnesses, ropes, and avalanche equipment have manufacturer lifespans regardless of condition or use. We won't repair life-safety equipment, and we won't extend a lifespan. If it's past date, retire it.

## Starting a claim

Any store, or contact support with photographs and proof of purchase.
