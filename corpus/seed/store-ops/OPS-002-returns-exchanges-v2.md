# Returns and Exchanges

## Standard window

Unworn merchandise with proof of purchase may be returned within **60 days** for a full refund to the original payment method.

Without proof of purchase, the return is processed as merchandise credit at the current selling price.

## Exceptions

| Category | Window |
|---|---|
| Technical apparel and outerwear | 60 days |
| Footwear, worn outdoors | 14 days, one exchange |
| Climbing hardware and safety equipment | 14 days, unopened only |
| Final sale and clearance | No returns |
| Gift cards | No returns |

## Refund method

Card purchases refund to the original card. Cash purchases above $100 refund by check from Accounts Payable within 15 business days.

Online purchases must be returned by mail and cannot be returned in store.

## Manager approval

Required for returns outside the window and returns above $250 without proof of purchase.
