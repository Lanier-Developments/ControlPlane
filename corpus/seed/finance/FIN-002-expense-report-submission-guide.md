# Expense Report Submission Guide

This guide covers how to submit. What is reimbursable and at what limit is defined in the travel and expense policy.

## Submitting

Expenses are submitted in Ledgerline. Each line requires a date, amount, category, and business purpose. "Client meeting" is not a business purpose; "lunch with Summit Outdoor buyer to review spring order" is.

Attach an itemized receipt where the policy requires one. A credit card summary is not an itemized receipt because it does not show what was purchased.

## Approval

Reports route to the submitter's manager, then to Accounts Payable. Managers have 5 business days to approve; after that the report escalates to the manager's manager automatically.

Accounts Payable reviews for policy compliance, not for business judgment. A compliant expense that a manager considers unnecessary is a conversation between the employee and the manager, not a rejection reason for Accounts Payable.

## Payment

Approved reports are paid in the next Friday disbursement run. Expect payment within 10 business days of manager approval.

## Common rejections

Missing itemized receipt. Expense submitted after the deadline stated in the policy. Personal items on a hotel folio, which must be split out before submission. Alcohol charged to a meal without a business purpose naming attendees.
