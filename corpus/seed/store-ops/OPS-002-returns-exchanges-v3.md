# Returns and Exchanges

## Standard window

Unworn merchandise with proof of purchase may be returned within **90 days** for a full refund to the original payment method. The window was 60 days under the previous version.

Without proof of purchase, the return is processed as merchandise credit at the current selling price, which may be lower than what the customer paid.

## Exceptions

| Category | Window |
|---|---|
| Technical apparel and outerwear | 90 days |
| Footwear, worn outdoors | 30 days, one exchange |
| Climbing hardware and safety equipment | 30 days, unopened only |
| Nutrition and consumables | Unopened, 90 days |
| Final sale and clearance | No returns |
| Gift cards | No returns |

Climbing and safety equipment is never restocked once opened, regardless of condition, because the store cannot verify its use history. This is a safety rule and a manager may not override it.

## Refund method

Card purchases refund to the original card. Cash purchases above $150 refund by check from Accounts Payable within 10 business days, not from the drawer. Online purchases returned in store refund to the original payment method.

## Manager approval

Required for: returns outside the window, returns above $500 without proof of purchase, and any third return by the same customer within 90 days.

## Fraud signals

Repeated no-receipt returns of the same category, returns of items with the tag reattached, and returns just inside a closing window. Log the pattern and notify loss prevention; do not confront the customer.
