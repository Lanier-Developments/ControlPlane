# 2026 Salary Bands

Bands apply to US-based full-time roles. Offers above the band maximum require VP of People approval.

| Role | Level | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineer | I | $92,000 | $118,000 |
| Senior Engineer | II | $148,000 | $182,000 |
| Senior Product Manager | II | $140,000 | $175,000 |
| Store Operations Manager | I | $68,000 | $86,000 |
