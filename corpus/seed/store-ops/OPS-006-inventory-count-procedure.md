# Inventory Count Procedure

## Cadence

| Count type | Frequency | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Cycle count | Weekly, Tuesday | One department, rotating |
| High-shrink count | Weekly, Tuesday | Electronics, optics, climbing hardware |
| Full physical | Quarterly | Entire store |
| Spot count | On demand | Single SKU under investigation |

Quarterly counts must be complete before business day 4 of the close, per the finance close schedule. A store that misses the window is estimated in the financials and flagged in the close package, which nobody enjoys explaining.

## Method

Count with the scanner, never from memory or the shelf label. Two associates count high-shrink categories independently and compare totals before submitting.

Do not adjust the system to match your count during the count. Submit the count and let the variance process run. Adjusting as you go destroys the variance signal, which is the entire point of counting.

## Variances

| Variance | Action |
|---|---|
| Under 1% of department units | Accept |
| 1% to 3% | Recount the affected category |
| Above 3% | Recount and notify regional manager |
| Any high-shrink category above 1% | Notify loss prevention |

A recount that confirms the original count is a real finding. Do not keep recounting until the number looks acceptable.

## Negative on-hand

A negative on-hand number is a system condition, not a counting error. Report it to Retail Systems rather than correcting it locally; the inventory service reconstructs the position from the movement ledger.
