# Returns and Exchanges — Customer Guide

## The short version

Bring it back within 90 days with your receipt and we'll refund the way you paid.

## What you need

A receipt, order confirmation, or the card you used. Without proof of purchase we can still take the return as store credit, at whatever the item currently sells for.

## Timing by category

Most items: 90 days. Footwear worn outdoors: 30 days, one exchange. Climbing and safety gear: 30 days, unopened only — once the packaging is open we can't take it back, because we can't verify how it's been used. Nutrition and consumables: 90 days, unopened. Final sale and clearance items and gift cards can't be returned.

## How you get your money back

Card purchases go back to the card you used. Cash purchases over $150 are refunded by check, which arrives within about 10 business days. Anything you bought online can be returned at any store or by mail.

## Online orders

Start a return in your account or bring the item to a store. If you mail it back, use the prepaid label in your order confirmation. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the item arriving at our warehouse.

## Gear that fails

If something fails in normal use, that's a warranty question, not a return. Talk to any store or contact support. Manufacturer warranties vary by brand and outlast our return window.
