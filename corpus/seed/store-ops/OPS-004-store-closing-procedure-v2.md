# Store Closing Procedure

Two associates minimum. **All closing work is paid time.** Associates clock out after closing tasks are complete, not at the posted closing hour.

This changed in the current version. The previous procedure had associates clock out at closing and complete the count afterward, which was wrong and has been corrected.

## Sequence

1. **Last call.** Announce 15 minutes before close. Do not lock doors on customers still in the store; serve them out.
2. **Lock the doors at the posted hour.** Customers already inside are finished normally.
3. **Register close.** Each drawer counted by the closing manager with a second associate present, reconciled to the point of sale report per the cash handling procedure.
4. **Deposit prep.** Deposit prepared and secured in the safe. No deposit is taken to the bank after dark by a single associate; the next banking day is always acceptable.
5. **Floor recovery.** Fold, face, and return go-backs. Fitting rooms cleared.
6. **Safety and security walk.** Fitting rooms, stockroom, restrooms, and office confirmed empty. Emergency exits secured but not blocked.
7. **Arm and leave together.** Associates walk out together and confirm the door is secured. Nobody is left alone in the parking lot.

## Time recording

Closing counts, recovery, and the security walk are all compensable. Any associate asked to work off the clock should report it immediately to the regional manager or the ethics line.

## Variances

A drawer variance is logged the same night. Do not hold a variance to resolve it in the morning; the record needs to reflect what was counted when it was counted.
