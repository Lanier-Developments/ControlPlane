# Customer Service Escalation

## Who resolves what

| Situation | Resolved by |
|---|---|
| Return within policy | Associate |
| Price match against a published competitor price | Associate |
| Return outside policy, under $500 | Store manager |
| Return outside policy, $500 or above | Regional manager |
| Product safety concern | Store manager, same day to loss prevention and Legal |
| Injury on premises | Store manager immediately, then regional manager and Legal |
| Media inquiry | Nobody in the store. Route to Communications |

## Price match

Match a published price from a competitor with a physical location within 50 miles, or from the competitor's own website. Match the item, not a similar one. Do not match marketplace listings, auction sites, or member-only pricing.

Price match is available at purchase and within 14 days after, with receipt.

## Injuries

Render aid, call emergency services if there is any doubt, and document the condition of the area with time and photographs before it changes. Do not speculate about cause with the customer, and do not offer to cover medical costs; both create problems Legal has to unwind later.

File the incident report the same day.

## Angry customers

Move the conversation off the sales floor. Listen fully before offering a resolution. An associate may offer any resolution within their authority without checking, and should say what they can do rather than what they cannot.

If a customer becomes threatening, disengage and involve the store manager or security. Associates are never required to absorb abuse.

## Media and social

Route every media inquiry to Communications. Do not respond to posts about the store, even to correct something plainly false.
