# Performance Review Cycle

Kestrel Ridge runs two formal review cycles per year: a mid-year review in June and a year-end review in December.

## Timeline

| Stage | Mid-year | Year-end |
|---|---|---|
| Self-assessment due | May 15 | November 15 |
| Manager ratings due | June 1 | December 1 |
| Reviews delivered | June 15 to June 30 | December 10 to December 20 |

## Ratings

Five ratings are used: Outstanding, Exceeds, Meets, Needs Improvement, and Unsatisfactory. Managers are expected to place no more than 15 percent of their team in Outstanding.

Employees rated Unsatisfactory enter a 90-day performance improvement plan.

## Peer feedback

Peer feedback is optional and collected only at year-end. Employees nominate up to five peers.
