# Store Hours Standard

Standard hours apply unless a location has an approved exception recorded in the store register.

| Day | Standard hours |
|---|---|
| Monday to Thursday | 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM |
| Friday | 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM |
| Saturday | 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM |
| Sunday | 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM |

## Reduced and extended days

Reduced hours: 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Applies on New Year's Day, Independence Day observed, and Christmas Eve.

Extended hours: 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Applies on the day after Thanksgiving only.

Stores are closed exactly two days a year, Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

## Exceptions

Mall locations follow mall hours where the lease requires it. Nine stores operate under mall hours; those exceptions are recorded in the store register and reviewed annually.

Seasonal extensions require regional manager approval and 14 days notice so scheduling and payroll can plan.

## Closing early

A store may close early only for a safety condition: severe weather, a facilities failure, or a security event. The store manager notifies the regional manager before closing, or immediately after if waiting would be unsafe.

Weather closures are posted to the website and the store's listing within 30 minutes. A closed store with hours still showing open generates customer complaints that outlast the weather.

## Staffing at open and close

Two associates minimum at open and at close, never one. This is a safety and cash-handling requirement and it is not waived for low-traffic days.
