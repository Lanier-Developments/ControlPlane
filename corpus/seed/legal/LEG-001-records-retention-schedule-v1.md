# Records Retention Schedule

This schedule is authoritative for how long Kestrel Ridge keeps records.

| Record type | Retention |
|---|---|
| Order and sales records | 7 years |
| Tax filings and supporting records | 7 years |
| Employee personnel file | 5 years after separation |
| Workplace investigation records | 3 years |
| Payroll records | 4 years |
| Vendor contracts | 5 years after expiration |
| Board minutes | Permanent |
| Customer account (closed) | 3 years after closure |
| Application logs | 90 days |
| Access and audit logs | 2 years |

## Destruction

Records at end of retention are destroyed on the quarterly destruction run. Destruction is suspended for anything covered by a legal hold.

## Questions

Route retention questions to Legal before deleting anything.
