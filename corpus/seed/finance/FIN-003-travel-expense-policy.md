# Travel and Expense Policy

## Lodging limits

| Travel type | Nightly hotel limit |
|---|---|
| Domestic | $225 |
| International | $325 |

## Meals

The meal per diem is $70 per day for domestic travel and $95 per day for international travel.

## Receipts

Itemized receipts are required for any single expense over $50. Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of the trip end date.
