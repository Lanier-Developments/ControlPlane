# Store 14 Bozeman Operating Supplement

Supplements the standard procedures for this location. Where this document is silent, the standard applies.

## Hours

Store 14 follows the standard hours schedule. It is not a mall location.

## Winter entrance

The entrance faces northeast and holds ice from October through April. Clearance is required at open and again at 2:00 PM on any day with precipitation or temperatures below freezing. Both clearances are logged with times.

## Ski and snowboard service

The service bench operates Wednesday through Sunday. Tunes and mounts are logged with the technician's initials; a binding mount without a recorded technician is not released to the customer.

Mount records are retained seven years with the order record, because a binding release failure can surface years later.

## Peak season

From December 15 through March 15 the store adds a Saturday opening associate at 8:00 AM for the pre-mountain rush. This is an approved standing exception to the staffing plan, not a weekly request.

## Closing

Associates walk out together to the side lot. The lot is unlit past the second row; park in the first two rows on closing shifts.

## Wildlife

Bear activity is reported behind the building in fall. Dumpster runs happen before dusk, never after close, and never alone.
