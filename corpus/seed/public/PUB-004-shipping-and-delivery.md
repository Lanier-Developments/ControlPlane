# Shipping and Delivery

## Options

| Method | Cost | Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | Free over $75, otherwise $7.95 | 4 to 7 business days |
| Expedited | $14.95 | 2 to 3 business days |
| Overnight | $29.95 | Next business day if ordered before 1:00 PM Pacific |
| Ship to store | Free | 3 to 6 business days |

Ridgeline Rewards members at Summit tier and above get free standard shipping with no minimum.

## Processing

Orders placed before 1:00 PM Pacific on a business day ship the same day. Orders placed on weekends or holidays process the next business day. Delivery estimates start when the order ships, not when it's placed.

During the holiday period, processing can take an extra day. Check the shipping deadlines posted on the site in December.

## Where we ship

All 50 US states. Alaska and Hawaii are standard shipping only and take 7 to 10 business days. We ship to APO and FPO addresses by standard shipping.

We don't currently ship internationally.

## Restrictions

Some items ship by ground only because of their contents, including fuel canisters, bear spray, and lithium batteries above a certain size. These items can't be expedited or shipped to Alaska, Hawaii, or APO and FPO addresses.

Oversized items like kayaks and rooftop boxes carry a freight surcharge shown at checkout.

## Tracking and problems

Tracking arrives by email when the order ships. If a package is marked delivered but isn't there, wait 24 hours, then contact support and we'll open a claim.
