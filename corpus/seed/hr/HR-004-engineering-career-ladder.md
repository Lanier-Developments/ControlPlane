# Engineering Career Ladder

The ladder describes expectations at each level. It does not contain compensation figures; those live in the salary band document, which is restricted to the total rewards team.

## Levels

**Engineer I.** Delivers well-defined tasks with review. Scope is a single component. Typically 0 to 2 years of experience.

**Engineer II.** Delivers features independently. Scope is a service or subsystem. Expected to write design notes for changes affecting other teams.

**Senior Engineer II.** Owns a subsystem end to end, including its on-call health. Scope extends across two or more teams. Mentors Engineer I and II. This is a terminal level; there is no expectation of further promotion.

**Staff Engineer.** Sets technical direction for an area. Scope is organizational. Resolves ambiguity that spans teams and quarters.

**Principal Engineer.** Influences direction across the engineering organization. One to two positions exist at any time.

## Promotion

Promotions are decided twice yearly, in the calibration sessions following the annual review and again in a April checkpoint. A promotion packet requires two sponsoring managers and three examples of sustained work at the target level. Time in level is not a criterion by itself; the shortest observed promotion from Engineer II to Senior Engineer II was 14 months.

Lateral moves between engineering and product do not reset level.
