# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Kestrel Ridge operates three work arrangements. Every employee is assigned one at hire, recorded in Wren, the HR system of record.

| Arrangement | On-site expectation | Eligible roles |
|---|---|---|
| On-site | 5 days per week | Retail, distribution center, facilities |
| Hybrid | 2 days per week in a company office | Corporate roles within 50 miles of an office |
| Remote | None | Corporate roles approved by a VP |

Hybrid employees choose their office days with their manager and keep them consistent within a quarter. Changing an arrangement requires a written request in Wren and VP approval; requests are reviewed on the first Monday of each month.

Remote employees must work from the state recorded in Wren. Working from another state for more than 21 consecutive days requires People Ops approval in advance, because it can create payroll tax obligations for the company.

Home office stipend: $500 on hire for remote employees, plus $250 every second year for equipment refresh. Hybrid employees receive $250 on hire. The stipend is claimed through the expense system and does not require itemized receipts under the $50 threshold rule.

Employees who relocate more than 50 miles must notify People Ops within 30 days. Compensation is not automatically adjusted for relocation within the United States.
