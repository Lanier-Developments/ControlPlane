# Store Cash Handling Procedure

Applies to all 42 retail locations.

## Opening

The opening manager counts the drawer with a second associate present. Standard opening float is $300 per register. Variances above $5 are logged in the store log and reported to the regional manager the same day.

## During the day

Cash above $1,000 in a register is dropped to the safe. Drops are logged with time, amount, and the initials of the associate making the drop.

No single associate may both count a drawer and approve its variance.

## Closing

The closing manager counts each drawer with a second associate present, reconciles to the point of sale report, and prepares the deposit. Deposits are made the next banking day.

Safe counts happen weekly, on Monday, by the store manager with a shift lead present.

## Variances

| Daily variance | Action |
|---|---|
| Under $5 | Log only |
| $5 to $50 | Log and notify regional manager |
| $51 to $250 | Regional manager investigates within 48 hours |
| Above $250 | Loss prevention notified immediately |

Three variances above $50 within a rolling 30 days triggers a loss prevention review regardless of individual amounts.

Counterfeit bills are retained, not returned to the customer, and reported to loss prevention with the transaction number.
