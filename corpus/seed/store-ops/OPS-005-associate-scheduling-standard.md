# Associate Scheduling Standard

## Posting

Schedules are posted 14 days in advance in the scheduling system. Several jurisdictions require this and it is the standard everywhere, not just where it is required.

Changes inside the 14 day window require the associate's agreement. An associate who declines a change inside the window faces no consequence, and a manager treating a decline as a performance issue is violating this standard.

## Shift rules

Minimum shift is 4 hours. Maximum is 10 hours without regional manager approval.

Minimum 10 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next. A close followed by an open the next morning is not scheduled; if it happens by swap, the associate may decline it.

Meal break of 30 minutes for shifts over 5 hours, taken before the fifth hour. Rest breaks of 15 minutes for every 4 hours worked. Breaks are not skipped to cover the floor; if coverage is that thin, the schedule is wrong.

## Availability

Associates submit availability in the scheduling system and may update it with 21 days notice. Managers schedule within stated availability. Repeatedly scheduling outside it is a management failure, not an associate problem.

## Swaps

Associates may swap shifts directly if both are trained for the role and the swap does not create overtime or break the 10 hour rule. Swaps are recorded in the system, never by verbal agreement, so the record shows who was actually scheduled.

## Changes after a complaint

A schedule change following an associate's complaint about pay or conditions requires regional manager review before it takes effect.
