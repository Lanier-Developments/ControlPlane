# Capital Expenditure Requests

A purchase is capital rather than operating expense when it has a useful life over one year and a unit cost of $5,000 or more. Below that threshold it is expensed, regardless of useful life.

| Asset class | Depreciation life |
|---|---|
| Store fixtures and displays | 7 years |
| Leasehold improvements | Lesser of 10 years or lease term |
| Computer hardware | 3 years |
| Warehouse equipment | 10 years |
| Vehicles | 5 years |
| Capitalized software | 5 years |

## Requesting

Capital requests are submitted in Ledgerline during the annual budget cycle. Unbudgeted requests require CFO approval and a written justification covering what breaks if the request waits until the next cycle.

A request must state the expected in-service date. Depreciation begins when the asset is placed in service, not when it is purchased, which matters for assets ordered in one fiscal year and installed in the next.

## Software

Internal software development costs are capitalized only during the application development stage. Planning, research, training, and post-implementation support are expensed. Engineering tracks the split through project codes; ask Finance before creating a new project code.

## Disposal

Asset disposals must be reported to Finance within 30 days so the asset register stays accurate. Stores disposing of fixtures during a remodel report through the regional manager.
