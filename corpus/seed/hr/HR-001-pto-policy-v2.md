# Paid Time Off Policy

Effective January 1, 2026, full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued evenly each pay period.

Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days into the following year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they are forfeited.

Requests of three or more consecutive days require manager approval at least two weeks in advance.
