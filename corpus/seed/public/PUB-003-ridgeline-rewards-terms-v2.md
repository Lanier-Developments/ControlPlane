# Ridgeline Rewards Program Terms

Free to join, in store or online.

## Earning

Earn 1 point per dollar spent on merchandise, before tax and after discounts. Gift card purchases don't earn points; spending the gift card does.

| Tier | Annual spend | Points per dollar | Other benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | $0 to $499 | 1 | Birthday offer |
| Summit | $500 to $1,999 | 1.25 | Free shipping, early sale access |
| Alpine | $2,000 and above | 1.5 | Free shipping, early access, free ski tuning |

Tier is evaluated on the calendar year and applies for the following year. Tiers do not downgrade mid-year.

## Redeeming

500 points converts to a $25 reward certificate. Certificates expire 90 days after issue and can be used on any non-clearance merchandise. Only one certificate per transaction.

## Points expiration

Points expire after 24 months of account inactivity. Any purchase resets the clock. The previous version expired points after 18 months.

## Returns

Points earned on a returned item are deducted. If a reward certificate was used on a returned order, the certificate is reissued with its original expiration date.

## The rest

One account per person. Accounts can't be combined, sold, or transferred. Points have no cash value. We can change these terms with 30 days notice posted here.

Closing your account forfeits any unredeemed points.
