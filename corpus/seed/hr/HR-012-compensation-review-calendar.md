# Compensation Review Calendar and Budget Guidance

Confidential. For the total rewards team. Do not share merit budget figures with managers before the December briefing.

## Calendar

| Stage | Date |
|---|---|
| Budget set by Finance | October 15 |
| Manager recommendations open | November 10 |
| Recommendations due | December 1 |
| Total rewards review | December 2 to December 12 |
| Manager briefing | December 15 |
| Increases effective | February 1 |

## Budget guidance

The 2026 merit budget is 3.2 percent of corporate base salary spend and 3.8 percent for retail, reflecting local wage pressure in the mountain west markets.

Promotion increases are funded separately at 0.6 percent of base spend and do not draw from the merit pool.

## Guardrails

No individual increase above 12 percent without VP of People approval. Employees below the band minimum are brought to minimum before merit is applied; this adjustment is funded outside the merit pool.

Employees rated Does Not Meet receive no merit increase. Employees on a performance improvement plan at the time of the review are held until the plan concludes.

Off-cycle increases require a written justification and are limited to retention cases with a documented competing offer.
