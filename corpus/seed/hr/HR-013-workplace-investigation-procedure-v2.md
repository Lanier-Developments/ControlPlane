# Workplace Investigation Procedure

Revised February 2026 after the outside counsel review. This version adds investigator
guidance and case-handling standards, and is maintained for the People Ops team; the
employee-facing summary of how to report a concern now lives in the Code of Conduct.

## Intake standards

Every report is acknowledged within two business days of receipt. The acknowledgment
names the People Ops contact handling the intake, explains the next steps, and must not
characterize the merits of the report.

Intake notes go in the case file, not email. A report received by a manager must be
forwarded to People Ops within one business day, whether or not the manager believes it
has merit.

## Investigator assignment

The assigned investigator must have no reporting relationship to the reporter, the
subject, or any named witness. For concerns naming a director or above, engage outside
counsel before the first interview. Investigations target closure within 21 calendar
days; the case file records the reason for any extension.

## Interviews and evidence

Interview the reporter first, the subject last. Collect documents before interviews
where possible — Wren records, schedules, messages — so interviews test accounts against
evidence rather than memory against memory.

## Closure

Outcome categories are substantiated, unsubstantiated, or inconclusive. The closure memo
records the standard applied (preponderance of the evidence), the findings, and the
corrective action. Reporter and subject are told the outcome category only. Retaliation
monitoring continues for six months after closure, with a calendar check at 30, 90 and
180 days.
