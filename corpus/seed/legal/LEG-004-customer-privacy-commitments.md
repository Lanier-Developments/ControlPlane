# Customer Privacy Commitments

What Kestrel Ridge collects, why, and what customers can ask us to do about it.

## What we collect

Account information you provide: name, email, shipping and billing address, phone number if you give one. Order history. Loyalty program activity. Website and app usage, including pages viewed and searches run.

We do not store full payment card numbers. Card transactions are handled by our payment processor, which returns a token we use for refunds and order lookup.

## Why

To fulfill orders, run the loyalty program, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve the store and site. We use usage data for our own analytics and product decisions.

## Sharing

We share data with carriers to ship orders, with our payment processor to take payment, and with service providers who work on our behalf under contract. We do not sell customer personal information.

## Your choices

Request a copy of your data, correction of it, or deletion of your account, through the privacy request form or by contacting support. We respond within 30 days.

Deleting an account removes your profile and identifiers. Order records are kept in de-identified form because tax and warranty obligations require it.

Marketing email can be turned off from any message or in account settings. Transactional messages about orders you placed continue.

## Retention

We keep records for the periods set in our retention schedule, generally 7 years for order records and 3 years after closure for account data.
