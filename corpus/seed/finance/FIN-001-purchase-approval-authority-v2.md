# Purchase Approval Authority

Every purchase commitment requires approval at or above the threshold for its total contract value, not its monthly or annual installment. A three-year contract at $40,000 per year is a $120,000 commitment.

| Total commitment | Approver |
|---|---|
| Up to $2,500 | Manager |
| $2,501 to $25,000 | Director |
| $25,001 to $100,000 | Vice President |
| $100,001 to $500,000 | Chief Financial Officer |
| Above $500,000 | CFO and Chief Executive Officer |

Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is a policy violation regardless of intent.

## Software and vendors

Any software purchase, including free tiers that process company data, requires a security review before approval. Route the request through the vendor intake form; the review takes 10 business days for a standard request.

Purchases above $25,000 require three written quotes or a documented sole-source justification.

## Purchase orders

Commitments above $10,000 require a purchase order issued before work begins. An invoice arriving without a matching purchase order is held by Accounts Payable and escalated to the requesting director.

Renewals are treated as new commitments. Auto-renewing contracts must be flagged in the vendor register 90 days before the renewal date.
