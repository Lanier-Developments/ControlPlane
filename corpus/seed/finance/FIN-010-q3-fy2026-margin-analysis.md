# Q3 FY2026 Margin Analysis

Confidential. Not for distribution outside Finance ahead of the board package.

## Summary

Gross margin for Q3 was 42.1 percent, down 130 basis points from the prior year quarter. Two drivers account for most of the decline.

Freight costs rose 18 percent year over year on the west coast lanes following the carrier consolidation. The logistics team has rebid the lanes with an expected effect in Q1 FY2027, not sooner.

Markdowns ran 260 basis points above plan in technical apparel, concentrated in the insulated layer category, where the warm October in the mountain markets left inventory heavy going into November.

## By channel

| Channel | Gross margin | vs plan |
|---|---|---|
| Retail stores | 44.3% | -80 bps |
| Online | 37.9% | -240 bps |
| Wholesale | 31.2% | +40 bps |

Online margin carries the shipping subsidy introduced in Q2. Excluding the subsidy, online margin would be approximately 41.5 percent.

## Outlook

Q4 margin is forecast at 43.5 percent, assuming markdowns return to plan and no further freight escalation. The forecast does not assume any benefit from the rebid lanes.

Inventory at quarter end was $87.4 million, 9 percent above the prior year on 4 percent sales growth.
