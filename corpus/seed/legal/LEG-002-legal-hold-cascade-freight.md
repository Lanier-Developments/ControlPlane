# Legal Hold Notice — Cascade Freight Dispute

Restricted. This notice and its existence are confidential. Do not forward, discuss outside the custodian list, or reference in any system that is not covered by the hold.

## Matter

Cascade Freight Partners has asserted claims arising from the west coast lane rebid concluded in February 2026, alleging that Kestrel Ridge used confidential rate information from the incumbent contract in the competitive process. Outside counsel is Harlan Reeve LLP.

## Scope

Preserve all records from November 1, 2025 forward relating to: the west coast lane rebid, rate negotiations with Cascade Freight Partners or any competing carrier, the freight cost analysis prepared for the Q3 margin review, and any communication referencing Cascade Freight by name or by the internal shorthand "CFP."

Preservation covers email, chat, documents, spreadsheets, calendar entries, and text messages on company or personal devices used for company business.

## Custodians

The named custodians are the VP of Distribution, the Director of Logistics, two logistics analysts, the Controller, and one FP&A analyst. Custodians receive this notice individually and must acknowledge within 5 business days.

## Effect on retention

This hold overrides the records retention schedule for all records within scope. Automated destruction has been suspended for the affected systems. Do not delete anything in scope, including drafts and superseded versions, until Legal releases the hold in writing.

Deleting material under hold is a serious matter with consequences beyond the company's control.

## Questions

Direct all questions to Legal. Do not discuss the matter with Cascade Freight personnel.
