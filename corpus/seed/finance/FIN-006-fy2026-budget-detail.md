# FY2026 Budget Detail by Department

Confidential. Department leaders receive their own line only; the consolidated view is restricted to Finance.

| Department | FY2026 budget | Change vs FY2025 |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | $18,400,000 | +11% |
| Distribution | $12,900,000 | +6% |
| Marketing | $9,200,000 | -4% |
| Finance | $5,700,000 | flat |
| People Ops | $4,100,000 | +2% |
| Legal | $3,300,000 | +18% |

Retail operations is budgeted separately in the store operations plan because it is driven by store count rather than headcount.

## Drivers

Engineering growth funds the platform replatform and 9 net new engineering positions. Legal growth reflects the addition of two in-house counsel positions and the compliance program.

Marketing's reduction reflects moving paid search spend into the affiliate program, which is carried as cost of sales rather than operating expense.

## Contingency

A $2,000,000 unallocated contingency sits at the corporate level. Releases require CFO approval and are reported to the board quarterly.

## Headcount

Total approved headcount for FY2026 is 1,847 including retail, up from 1,791. Corporate headcount is 312.
