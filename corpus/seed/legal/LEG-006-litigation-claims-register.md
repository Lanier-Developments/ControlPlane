# Litigation and Claims Register

Restricted. Contains reserve estimates and assessments prepared in anticipation of litigation. Not for distribution outside Legal.

## Active matters

**Cascade Freight Partners — contract dispute.** Filed April 2026, District of Oregon. Claims arising from the west coast lane rebid. Outside counsel Harlan Reeve LLP. Reserve $1,400,000. Assessment: defensible on the merits; the rate information at issue was independently developed. Mediation scheduled September 2026.

**Alderidge v. Kestrel Ridge — premises liability.** Filed January 2026, Colorado. Customer injury at Store 27 alleging inadequate snow clearance at the entrance. Reserve $175,000. Assessment: likely settles within reserve. Insurance carrier is defending under a reservation of rights.

**Wage and hour class claim — closing procedures.** Demand letter received May 2026, California. Alleges unpaid time for closing counts across 6 California stores. Reserve $900,000. Assessment: the exposure is real. The same practice was substantiated in a single-employee investigation earlier this year, and the corrected payment in that matter is discoverable. Store Operations has since changed the closing procedure.

**Trailhead Gear — trademark opposition.** Opposition filed against the Kestrel Ridge application for a sub-brand mark. Reserve $60,000. Assessment: coexistence agreement likely.
