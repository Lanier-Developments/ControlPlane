# Fiscal Calendar and Close Schedule

Kestrel Ridge operates on a fiscal year ending January 31. Fiscal 2026 runs February 1, 2026 through January 31, 2027.

## Quarters

| Quarter | Period |
|---|---|
| Q1 | February 1 to April 30 |
| Q2 | May 1 to July 31 |
| Q3 | August 1 to October 31 |
| Q4 | November 1 to January 31 |

The retail calendar's peak falls in Q4, which is why the fiscal year ends after the holiday season rather than in December.

## Monthly close

Close runs for 6 business days after month end. Accruals are due on business day 2, intercompany reconciliation on day 3, and the close package is issued on day 6.

Expense reports submitted after the last business day of the month land in the following period.

## Quarterly close

Quarter close runs 9 business days. Store inventory counts must be complete before day 4; a store that misses the count window is estimated and flagged in the package.

## Budget cycle

Department budgets are drafted in September, reviewed in October, and locked by November 15 for the following fiscal year. Mid-year reforecasts happen once, after Q2 close.
