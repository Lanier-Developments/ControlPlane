# Store Locations and Services

42 stores across the western and mountain states. Find hours and directions for a specific store on its page; hours vary at mall locations.

## Typical hours

Monday to Thursday 10 to 8, Friday 10 to 9, Saturday 9 to 9, Sunday 10 to 6. Nine of our stores are in malls and follow mall hours instead.

We're closed two days a year: Thanksgiving and Christmas Day. We close early on New Year's Day, July 3rd, and Christmas Eve, and open early the day after Thanksgiving.

## Services

**Ski and snowboard shop.** Tunes, waxing, and binding mounts at 14 locations, including Bozeman and Truckee. Turnaround is usually 3 days, longer in January.

**Boot fitting.** Free with a boot purchase at stores with a certified fitter. Appointments recommended on weekends.

**Climbing wall.** Demonstration walls at 6 stores, open Thursday through Sunday with staff present. Everyone climbing signs a waiver, and anyone under 18 needs a parent or guardian to sign.

**Pack fitting and rentals.** Backpack fitting at all locations. Weekend rental for packs, snowshoes, and avalanche gear at mountain-market stores; rental fees apply to purchase if you buy the same item within 14 days.

**Ship to store.** Order online, pick up free at any location.

## Accessibility

All stores are wheelchair accessible. Service animals are welcome. If you need assistance during your visit, ask any associate or call ahead.
