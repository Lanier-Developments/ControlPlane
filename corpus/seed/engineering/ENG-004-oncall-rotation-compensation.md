# On-Call Rotation and Compensation

## Rotation

Tier 1 services run a primary and secondary rotation, one week each, handing over Wednesday at 10:00 AM local time. Handover is a live conversation, not a message: the outgoing engineer walks the incoming one through open issues and anything deferred.

A rotation requires at least 5 engineers. Teams below 5 share a rotation with a partner team rather than running a 4-person rotation.

## Expectations

Primary acknowledges a page within 15 minutes. Secondary is paged if primary does not acknowledge. If secondary does not acknowledge within 15 minutes, the page escalates to the Platform Engineering lead.

On-call engineers are not expected to carry feature work during their week. Managers who schedule sprint commitments for an on-call engineer are doing it wrong.

## Compensation

| Rotation | Weekly stipend |
|---|---|
| Tier 1 primary | $700 |
| Tier 1 secondary | $350 |
| Tier 2 | $250 |

Stipends are paid in the payroll cycle following the rotation week. Engineers paged more than 4 times outside business hours in a week receive an additional $200 disruption payment.

## After a rough night

An engineer paged between midnight and 5:00 AM takes the following morning off with no PTO deduction. This is not a request; the manager confirms it happened.
