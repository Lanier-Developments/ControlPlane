# Tuition Reimbursement

Kestrel Ridge reimburses approved coursework up to **$3,500 per calendar year** for full-time employees. Part-time employees are not eligible.

## Eligibility

Available after 12 months of continuous employment.

## Approval

Coursework must be approved in Wren before the course start date and must relate to the employee's current role.

## Reimbursement

Reimbursement requires a grade of B or better. Submit the grade report and receipt within 30 days of course completion.

## Repayment

Employees who leave for any reason within 24 months of a reimbursement repay 100 percent of amounts received.

Professional certifications are not covered by this policy.
