# Code of Conduct

Every employee acknowledges this document at hire and annually thereafter during the compliance window in February.

## Conflicts of interest

Employees must disclose outside employment, board seats, and financial interests in any Kestrel Ridge vendor or competitor. Disclosure is made in Wren and reviewed by People Ops and Legal. Ownership below 1 percent of a publicly traded company does not require disclosure.

## Gifts

Gifts from vendors valued above $75 must be declined or surrendered to People Ops. Meals during business travel are not gifts. No employee involved in a vendor selection may accept anything of value from a bidding vendor during the selection period.

## Reporting

Concerns may be raised with a manager, People Ops, or the anonymous ethics line operated by a third party. Reports to the ethics line are reviewed within 5 business days.

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith is itself a violation, independent of whether the original concern is substantiated.

## Consequences

Violations are handled case by case. Sanctions range from coaching through termination. Findings are documented in the investigation record and retained per the records retention schedule.
