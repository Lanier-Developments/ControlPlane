# Involuntary Termination Procedure

This procedure applies to terminations for performance, conduct, or position elimination. It is restricted to People Ops and Legal.

## Before the decision

A People Ops partner reviews the documentation record: the performance improvement plan and its outcome, or the investigation findings for conduct cases. Legal reviews any case involving an employee who has raised a complaint or taken protected leave in the preceding 12 months.

Position eliminations affecting 10 or more employees at one site trigger separate notice obligations; Legal determines applicability before any notification.

## Severance

| Tenure | Severance |
|---|---|
| Under 1 year | 2 weeks |
| 1 to 4 years | 4 weeks |
| 5 to 9 years | 8 weeks |
| 10 years or more | 12 weeks |

Severance for position elimination follows the table and requires a signed release. Severance is not offered for terminations for cause.

## Day of

System access is revoked at the start of the meeting, not before, so the employee is not alerted by a lockout. Badge access, Wren, email, and VPN are revoked together through the offboarding workflow.

Final pay follows state requirements; several states require payment on the final day.

Managers do not announce the departure before People Ops confirms the meeting has concluded.
