# Tuition Reimbursement

Kestrel Ridge reimburses approved coursework up to **$6,000 per calendar year** for full-time employees and $2,000 for part-time employees working 20 or more hours per week.

## Eligibility

Available after 6 months of continuous employment. Part-time eligibility is new in this version; the previous policy covered full-time employees only.

## Approval

Coursework must be approved in Wren before the course start date. Approval requires a statement of how the coursework relates to the employee's current role or a role in the published career ladder. Degree programs are approved once, not per course.

## Reimbursement

Reimbursement requires a passing grade of C or better, or Pass in a pass/fail course. Submit the grade report and receipt within 60 days of course completion.

## Repayment

Employees who leave voluntarily within 12 months of a reimbursement repay 50 percent of amounts received in that period. Employees terminated without cause do not repay. The previous policy required 100 percent repayment within 24 months; the reduced obligation applies to reimbursements issued on or after January 1, 2026.

Professional certifications count as coursework. Exam fees are covered; study materials are not.
