# Store Closing Procedure

Two associates minimum.

## Sequence

1. **Last call.** Announce 15 minutes before close.
2. **Lock the doors at the posted hour.**
3. **Clock out.** Associates clock out at the posted closing hour.
4. **Register close.** Each drawer counted by the closing manager with a second associate present.
5. **Deposit prep.** Deposit prepared and secured in the safe.
6. **Floor recovery.** Fold, face, and return go-backs.
7. **Security walk.** Fitting rooms, stockroom, restrooms, and office confirmed empty.
8. **Arm and leave together.**

## Variances

A drawer variance is logged the same night or the following morning.
