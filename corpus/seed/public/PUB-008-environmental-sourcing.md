# Environmental and Sourcing Commitments

What we've actually done, and what we haven't.

## Materials

62 percent of our branded apparel by volume now uses recycled or responsibly sourced primary fabric, up from 41 percent in 2023. Our target is 85 percent by 2030.

All down is certified to the Responsible Down Standard. All wool comes from suppliers audited against the Responsible Wool Standard.

We finished removing intentionally added PFAS from our branded apparel in the 2026 spring line. Some third-party brands we carry still use it; their product pages say so.

## Repair over replacement

Our repair program extended the life of roughly 14,000 items last year. Repair is the most effective thing we do, and it's the least visible.

## Stores and operations

All 42 stores run on LED lighting. 11 stores have on-site solar. Our Bend distribution center has been landfill-free since 2024; the Reno facility has not, and we expect to get there in 2027.

## Shipping

Orders ship in recycled and recyclable packaging. We don't offer carbon-neutral shipping as a checkout option, because we'd rather reduce emissions than buy offsets against them.

## What we haven't figured out

Our supply chain emissions are the majority of our footprint and the part we measure least well. We report scope 1 and 2 emissions annually and are still building a credible scope 3 picture.

We're not going to claim a net-zero date we don't know how to reach.

## Reporting

Our annual impact report is published each June.
