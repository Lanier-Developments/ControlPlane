# Performance Review Cycle

Kestrel Ridge runs one formal review cycle per year, replacing the twice-yearly cycle used through 2025.

## Timeline

| Stage | Window |
|---|---|
| Self-assessment opens | September 1 |
| Self-assessment due | September 21 |
| Peer feedback due | September 30 |
| Manager ratings due | October 20 |
| Calibration sessions | October 21 to November 7 |
| Reviews delivered | November 17 to December 5 |

## Ratings

Four ratings are used: Exceeds, Meets, Partially Meets, and Does Not Meet. There is no forced distribution, but calibration sessions require a manager to justify any rating of Exceeds with two specific examples of impact beyond the role's scope.

Employees rated Does Not Meet enter a 60-day performance improvement plan, drafted by the manager and reviewed by a People Ops partner before delivery.

## Peer feedback

Each employee nominates three peers; the manager may add up to two. Peer feedback is summarized rather than shared verbatim, and is never attributed to a named reviewer in the delivered review.

Compensation decisions follow the review cycle but are governed separately. Ratings do not map to a fixed increase percentage.
