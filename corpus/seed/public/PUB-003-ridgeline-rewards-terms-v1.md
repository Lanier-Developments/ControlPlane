# Ridgeline Rewards Program Terms

Free to join, in store or online.

## Earning

Earn 1 point per dollar spent on merchandise, before tax and after discounts.

| Tier | Annual spend | Points per dollar | Other benefits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | $0 to $749 | 1 | Birthday offer |
| Summit | $750 and above | 1.25 | Free shipping |

## Redeeming

400 points converts to a $20 reward certificate. Certificates expire 60 days after issue.

## Points expiration

Points expire after 18 months of account inactivity.

## The rest

One account per person. Points have no cash value.
