# Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue 15 days of paid time off per calendar year, accrued evenly each pay period.

Unused PTO does not carry over. Any balance remaining on December 31 is forfeited.

Requests of three or more consecutive days require manager approval at least two weeks in advance.
