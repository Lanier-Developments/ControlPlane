# Careers at Kestrel Ridge

About 1,800 people work here, most of them in our 42 stores. Open positions are posted on our careers site.

## How hiring works

Apply online. A recruiter reviews applications and responds either way, usually within two weeks.

Retail roles: a phone conversation, then an in-store interview with the store manager.

Corporate roles: a recruiter conversation, a hiring manager interview, and a panel of two to four people. Engineering roles include a technical conversation about work you've actually done; we don't ask candidates to solve puzzles on a whiteboard.

We tell you the salary range for the role in the first conversation.

## Working here

Full-time employees get paid time off, health coverage, a retirement plan, tuition support for job-related coursework, and a merchandise discount. Part-time associates working 20 or more hours a week are eligible for several of these, including tuition support.

Specific benefit details are shared during the hiring process and in the benefits guide provided at offer.

## Locations

Corporate roles are based in Bend, Oregon, with hybrid and remote arrangements depending on the role. Distribution roles are in Bend and Reno. Retail roles are at the store you apply to.

## Equal opportunity

We hire without regard to race, color, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, national origin, age, disability, veteran status, or any other protected characteristic.

If you need an accommodation during the hiring process, tell your recruiter. It won't affect your candidacy.

## Watch out for fraud

We never ask candidates for payment, bank details, or identification documents before a written offer. Our recruiters email from kestrelridge.example addresses only. Report anything suspicious to careers@kestrelridge.example.
