# Workplace Investigation Summary 2026-03

Restricted. Contains personal information about identified individuals. Do not redistribute.

## Parties

Reporting party: Nora Feldkamp, employee ID KR-40118, Store 27 (Boulder), shift lead.
Subject: Trent Ashby, employee ID KR-31904, Store 27, assistant manager.
Investigator: Renata Oyelaran, employee relations partner.

Contact of record for the reporting party during the investigation: 541-555-0173.

## Allegation

Filed March 9, 2026 through the ethics line. The reporting party alleged that scheduling assignments were altered after she raised a concern about unpaid time spent on closing counts, and that two shifts were removed from her schedule in the week following.

## Findings

Scheduling records confirm two shifts were removed on March 11. The assistant manager stated the change followed a coverage request from another associate; no record of that request exists in the scheduling system.

The investigator found the retaliation allegation substantiated on the balance of evidence. The underlying wage concern was referred to Payroll and resolved by a corrected payment of 3.5 hours.

## Outcome

The assistant manager received a final written warning and was removed from schedule administration for Store 27. The reporting party's schedule was restored. Both parties were informed of the non-retaliation policy in writing.

Retained for seven years per the records retention schedule.
