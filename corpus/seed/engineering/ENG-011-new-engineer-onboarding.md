# New Engineer Onboarding

## Week one

Day 1: accounts, laptop, and access to the engineering handbook. Do not request production access in week one; you will not need it and you cannot get it.

Day 2 to 3: run the local development environment for your team's service. The goal is a passing test suite on your own machine by the end of day 3. If that takes longer, the setup documentation is broken and fixing it is your first contribution.

Day 4 to 5: ship something small. Every team maintains a list of starter issues. A merged pull request in week one is the target, and it is the onboarding buddy's job to make that possible.

## First month

Pair with your onboarding buddy for at least 4 hours per week. Attend one incident review even if your team was not involved; it is the fastest way to learn how the systems actually fail.

Shadow an on-call rotation before joining one. Nobody carries a pager alone before their second month.

## Access

Access is granted by role through the standard request, not by copying another engineer's permissions. "Give me what Priya has" is not a valid request and will be rejected.

## Ninety days

By day 90 you should be able to: deploy your team's service, roll it back, find the dashboard for it, name its upstream and downstream dependencies, and declare an incident without asking permission.
