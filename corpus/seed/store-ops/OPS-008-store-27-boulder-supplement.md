# Store 27 Boulder Operating Supplement

Supplements the standard procedures for this location. Where this document is silent, the standard applies.

## Hours

Store 27 operates under mall hours: Monday to Saturday 10:00 AM to 9:00 PM, Sunday 11:00 AM to 6:00 PM. This is one of the nine recorded mall-hours exceptions.

## Winter entrance

The entrance faces north and holds ice from November through April. Clearance is required at open and again at 2:00 PM on any day with precipitation or temperatures below freezing. Both clearances are logged with times.

This location has an active premises claim arising from a winter entrance condition. Clearance logging is not optional here.

## Climbing wall

The demonstration wall operates Thursday through Sunday with a certified associate present. It is closed at all other times and the gate is locked, not merely closed.

Waivers are required for every user including children, signed by a parent or guardian. No waiver, no wall, no exceptions for regulars.

## Closing

Mall security escorts associates to the parking structure after close. Call the security desk at close rather than walking out unescorted.

## Staffing note

Following the March 2026 procedure review, all schedule changes at this location within the posting window require regional manager approval before taking effect, not just associate agreement.
