# 2026 Holiday Schedule

Corporate offices are closed on the days below. Retail stores follow the store hours schedule and operate on most of these days; retail associates who work a company holiday receive premium pay as described below.

| Holiday | Date | Corporate | Stores |
|---|---|---|---|
| New Year's Day | January 1 | Closed | Open, reduced hours |
| Memorial Day | May 25 | Closed | Open |
| Independence Day (observed) | July 3 | Closed | Open, reduced hours |
| Labor Day | September 7 | Closed | Open |
| Thanksgiving | November 26 | Closed | Closed |
| Day after Thanksgiving | November 27 | Closed | Open, extended hours |
| Christmas Eve | December 24 | Closed | Open, reduced hours |
| Christmas Day | December 25 | Closed | Closed |

Stores are closed on exactly two days per year: Thanksgiving and Christmas Day.

## Floating holidays

Full-time employees receive 2 floating holidays per calendar year, taken with manager approval. Floating holidays do not carry over and are not paid out at termination.

## Premium pay

Retail associates scheduled on a company holiday receive 1.5 times their base rate for hours worked that day. Salaried employees do not receive premium pay.
