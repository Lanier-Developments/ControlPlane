# Workplace Investigation Procedure

This procedure explains what happens after an employee reports a workplace concern — harassment, discrimination, retaliation, safety, or policy violations. It applies to every Kestrel Ridge location.

## Reporting

Report a concern to any of the following, whichever you are most comfortable with:

- Your manager, or any manager
- People Ops directly (peopleops@ inbox or in person)
- The anonymous ethics line, available 24/7

No one needs permission to make a report, and no one may be asked to withdraw one.

## What happens next

Every report is acknowledged within three business days of receipt. The acknowledgment names the People Ops contact handling the intake and explains the next steps.

People Ops assesses whether the report requires a formal investigation. If it does, an investigator is assigned who has no reporting relationship to anyone involved. Most investigations conclude within 30 calendar days; if one runs longer, the reporter is told why.

## Confidentiality and non-retaliation

Information is shared only with people who need it to investigate or act on findings. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith, or participates in an investigation, is itself a violation of the Code of Conduct and grounds for termination.

## Outcomes

When an investigation closes, the reporter and the subject are each told the outcome category — substantiated, unsubstantiated, or inconclusive — but not the details of the other party's interviews. Discipline, where warranted, follows the involuntary termination procedure or lesser corrective action.
