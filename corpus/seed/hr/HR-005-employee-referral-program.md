# Employee Referral Program

Any employee may refer a candidate through Wren. Referral bonuses are paid after the referred hire completes 90 days of employment.

| Role category | Bonus |
|---|---|
| Retail associate | $500 |
| Store leadership | $1,500 |
| Corporate individual contributor | $2,500 |
| Engineering and data | $4,000 |
| Director and above | $6,000 |

## Rules

The referral must be logged before the candidate applies, or within 5 business days of the application. Recruiters cannot claim referrals for candidates in their own requisitions.

Hiring managers are not eligible for a bonus on a hire into their own team. Members of the talent acquisition team are not eligible for any referral bonus.

Bonuses are paid in the payroll cycle following the 90-day mark and are treated as taxable income. If the referred employee leaves before 90 days, no bonus is paid; there is no clawback once a bonus has been paid.

A candidate who applied independently within the previous 12 months is not eligible for referral. Ties between two referrers are resolved in favor of whoever logged first.
